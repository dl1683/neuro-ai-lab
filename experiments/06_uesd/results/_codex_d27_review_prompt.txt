D27 tested encoder degradation across 3 seeds (42, 2024, 1337) at L=8. Cross-attention ablation results:
- seed=42: +28.4% accuracy delta (cross-attn helps enormously)
- seed=2024: +12.6% delta

- The 5.8-28.4% range suggests cross-attention importance is NOT universal — it varies dramatically by seed
- But noise equalization suggests the MECHANISM is universal — it just manifests differently in clean conditions
- Under perturbation, the channel saturates to a consistent contribution level
