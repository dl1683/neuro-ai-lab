# Claim Evidence Ledger

This ledger maps the repo's scientific claims to concrete artifacts. It is meant to prevent overclaiming and make the evidence trail auditable.

## Status labels

- `established`: directly supported by replicated result artifacts in this repo.
- `supported`: supported by current experiments but still narrow in scope.
- `mixed`: evidence is partially positive and partially limiting.
- `negative`: tested and not supported in its current form.
- `open`: plausible but not yet tested enough.

## Current strongest evidence

The strongest constructive result is the CIFAR-10 CNN 99% capacity reserve sweep:

- magnitude after fine-tuning: `0.32315`
- global SynFlow after fine-tuning: `0.09760`
- reserve `0.60` after fine-tuning: `0.34875`
- delta vs magnitude: `+0.02560`
- paired wins vs magnitude: `4/4`

The strongest limitation is TinyResNet 99%:

- magnitude after fine-tuning: about `0.2423` in the transfer run
- reserve `0.60` after fine-tuning: about `0.2132`
- dead outputs are almost eliminated, but accuracy still trails magnitude

Interpretation: capacity constraints solve circuit death, but residual architectures need a better definition of route quality than output liveness.

## Current public claim boundary

Safe claim:

> Severe global saliency pruning can create hidden topological cutsets. Path-capacity constraints prevent this collapse and can improve extreme-sparsity recovery in CNN settings, but current output-count reserves are not yet sufficient for harder residual-route regimes.

Updated synthesis:

> Severe pruning can create hidden circuit cutsets. Capacity constraints inspired by circuit viability prevent these failures and can improve extreme-sparsity recovery across CNN and residual settings. The evidence supports a hierarchy: preserve liveness first, then route-family balance and degeneracy.

Unsafe claim:

> Path-Capacity Pruning solves pruning generally.
