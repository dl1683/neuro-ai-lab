# Project rules

**Read `AGENTS.md` before doing anything in this repository — it is the binding rulebook for all agents here (behavior, gates, philosophy, validation).** Then read `STATUS.md` for current state. These two files override defaults and historical documents.

Quick contract: result JSONs and logged writeups are immutable evidence; frozen lines are not extended without a reopening decision in `STATUS.md`; every block of work ends with the five validation commands in `AGENTS.md` §7 passing; Codex (the real `codex` CLI) is the senior authority at design, pre-launch, evidence, and PR gates.
